Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2025

CiwAI Smart Digital (“we”, “our”, “us”) builds and distributes mobile applications including, but not limited to, AllNetTools and GarageCars (“Apps”). This Privacy Policy explains what information our Apps collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our Apps do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, or precise location.

Information Collected via Third-Party Services (Ads)

Our Apps use third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdMob, to display ads. As part of ad delivery and measurement, these services may collect:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • App usage data related to ad impressions and interactions
  • Approximate location inferred by the ad network (if enabled by the OS)

Data is used for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and improving ads, and may be processed according to the third party’s privacy policy. Learn more here: Google Ads & AdMob Policy.

All transfers occur over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

App-Specific Notes

  • AllNetTools: requires Internet access to perform network utilities (e.g., ping, DNS lookup, speed test). No personal data is uploaded to our servers.
  • GarageCars: stores vehicle records locally on your device (using on-device storage). We do not receive your database contents.

Your Choices & Controls

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or limit ad personalization from your device settings (e.g., Settings → Privacy → Ads on Android).
  • Permissions: You can grant or revoke app permissions anytime in system settings.
  • Uninstall: You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.

Children’s Privacy

Our Apps are intended for general/adult audiences and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data Security

We do not operate servers that store your personal data for these Apps. Third-party providers (e.g., AdMob) secure their processing in accordance with their policies.

International Transfers

Ad networks may process data on servers located in multiple countries. Such processing is governed by those providers’ privacy policies and applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
